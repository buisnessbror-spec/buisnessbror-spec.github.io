ChatWentCrazy

ChatWentCrazy is a short-form clip channel. We post funny and exciting moments from livestreams as vertical clips on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, mostly as part of official streamer clipping programs.

About the ChatWentCrazy publishing tool

The "clipping" app is a private tool operated by ChatWentCrazy for its own accounts only. It uploads clips that ChatWentCrazy has edited to ChatWentCrazy's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account, with a title, caption and hashtags. It does not post for other people, does not read other users' data, and has no public sign-up.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the "clipping" publishing tool operated by ChatWentCrazy (contact: buisness.bror@gmail.com).

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by ChatWentCrazy, to publish to ChatWentCrazy's own accounts. There are no other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to the ChatWentCrazy channel (youtube.upload) and to read that channel's own name and video status (youtube.readonly).
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the ChatWentCrazy Instagram account and read their links.
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to the ChatWentCrazy TikTok account and read its own basic profile.

The tool does not access, collect or store data about any other person, viewer or account.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on ChatWentCrazy's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are used solely to upload ChatWentCrazy's clips and confirm they were published. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred anywhere except to the platform it belongs to.

ChatWentCrazy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access on Google, Meta or TikTok invalidates its token, and the local token files are then deleted. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email buisness.bror@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date.
